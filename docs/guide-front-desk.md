# Front desk guide

For the secretary or receptionist who registers people, enrolls them, and takes payments. Screens and button names are as
they appear in the portal. Words like "class" and "student" may be different at your institution: an administrator can
rename them, and you will see their words.

You can only see and change what belongs to your branch. If you can't find someone, they may be registered at another branch.

## Signing in

Open the portal address, enter your email and password, and press **Sign in**. Your first sign-in comes from an invitation
email: follow its link to choose a password. If you forget your password, use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. After a
few wrong attempts the account is locked for a short while; wait and try again.

Sign out with **Sign out** (top right), especially on a shared computer.

## The home page

It shows your day at a glance: how much was collected today, instalments due today and overdue, new applications, and the
classes starting soon with how full they are. Each box is a link to the screen behind it.

## Someone applied on the website

People who pre-register on the website appear under **Admissions**, marked **Website** under "Heard about us", with a
reference number like `APP-2609-0001` (they were emailed the same number).

1. Open **Admissions** and find them (search by name, phone or reference).
2. Open the application. Call them, and press **Mark as contacted**.
3. When they are ready to join, press **Make an offer**, then in **Register as a student** press **Register student**.
   If the page warns that someone with the same phone, email or a very similar name is already registered, look at the
   list: press **This is them** to link to the existing person instead of creating a second record, or **This is someone
   new, register anyway** if it really is a different person.
4. Press **Open the student record**.

## Someone walks in

Press **New applicant** in **Admissions**, or go straight to **Students** and press **Register student**. Enter their names,
phone, gender and branch. Add a parent or guardian under **Guardians and contacts** for anyone who is a minor, and tick
**Pays the fees** for whoever pays: fee reminders go to that person.

## Enrolling a student in a class

1. Open **Classes** and open the class (check the shift, dates and how many seats are left).
2. In **Enroll a student**, search for them and press **Enroll**. If the class is full the button says **Add to waitlist**,
   and they move up automatically when a seat frees.
3. Some courses need an earlier course first; if the system says the prerequisites are not met, they need to complete
   that one first.

Enrolling is safe to repeat: pressing twice, or two people pressing at once, still gives one enrollment and never more
students than seats.

## Fees and payments

1. Open the student. Under **Fees**, press **Create invoice** next to the class. The fee comes from the course and the
   student's category, split into instalments by the institution's payment plan.
2. Open the invoice and press **Record a payment**. Enter **Amount received** and **How** (cash, bank transfer or cheque). For
   a bank transfer or cheque, type the reference from the slip.
3. Press **Record payment and issue receipt**. The receipt number appears. Payments always pay the oldest instalment first,
   and the system refuses more than is owed.

**Printing a receipt:** on the invoice press **Receipt** next to the payment. Choose **PDF (A5)**, **Thermal (80 mm)** for a
receipt printer, or **Print this page**.

**If something was entered wrongly:** a recorded payment can't be edited or deleted. On the invoice press **Request void** and
say why; an administrator approves it, and a void keeps its receipt number. Then record the payment correctly. To give a
discount, press **Ask for a discount** and choose the type, amount and reason. It takes effect when an administrator approves.
Track requests under **Billing > Approvals**.

## Student ID cards

Open the student and, under **ID card**, press **Issue card**, then open the PDF and print it. Issuing a replacement
cancels the old card: it will no longer verify.

## Things you can't do (and who can)

- Issue or revoke certificates: coordinators issue, administrators revoke.
- Approve discounts and voids, change settings, invite staff, see revenue reports: administrators.
- Record results: coordinators.

If a button you expect isn't there, you probably don't have permission for it: ask an administrator.

## When something looks wrong

Note the time and what you were doing, and tell an administrator. Every change in the system is recorded with who made it, so
it can be traced. Never share your password: anything done under your name is your responsibility.
